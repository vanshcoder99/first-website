Help

How To Get Started?

Welcome to Money Minder! Our platform helps you manage your finances by tracking your expenses and budgeting effectively. To get started, create an account by clicking on the "Sign Up" button and follow the prompts to set up your profile.

How To Navigate?

Learn how to navigate the dashboard and menu to access different features.

How To Add Expenses?

To add a new expense, click on the "My monthly expenditure" button on the dashboard. Enter the amount, date, category, and any tags you want to associate with the expense. Save the entry to add it to your records.

How To Manage Expenses?

You can view your expenses by clicking on the "My expenses" tab in the menu. Use the search bar to find specific entries, and click on an expense to edit or delete it.
